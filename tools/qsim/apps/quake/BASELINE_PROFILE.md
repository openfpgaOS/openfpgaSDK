# Baseline profile: openfpgaOS Quake on os25 (qsim)

> **Preserved measurement report.** Produced with qsim before it moved into the
> SDK (`tools/qsim/`), during the 2026-09 Quake optimization round. Absolute
> paths and `runs/`, `analysis/` and `review/` directory references below point
> at that session's scratch directories and no longer exist; the ELF sha256s,
> the workload definitions and the numbers do. The patches this report is about
> live in the Quake port repository under `tools/quake-opt-2026-09/`. Reproduce
> any of it with `tools/qsim/run.sh` / `gate.sh` (see `apps/quake/README.md`).

Generated by `analysis/make_report.py` from `runs/baseline-demo{1,2,3}` (canonical `run.sh` runs) and the per-PC class dumps in `runs/baseline-demoN/pcx/`. Every number below comes from those files; rerun the script after re-running the workload.

ELF under test: `/home/alberto/Repos/Quake/build/opt-20260915/baseline-obj/app.elf`, sha256 `0d7bd13e1721e62c78a3d5e03792d81d115006de8584234849b0197c6894b546`.

Workload: `quake.elf +timedemo demoN +host_framerate 0.1`, registered id1 PAKs, GPU raster model on, `--profile` (os25 timing model). "Cycles" means the model's `est_cycles`: instructions plus attributed penalty cycles. It is a relative A/B metric, not a cycle-accurate prediction (see [Anomalies and caveats](#14-anomalies-and-model-caveats)).

## 0. Summary

* All three demos finish with no traps: demo1 `969 frames  96.9 seconds  10.0 fps`, demo2 `985 frames  98.5 seconds  10.0 fps`, demo3 `1090 frames 109.0 seconds  10.0 fps`. Together that is 3,137 presented frames, 5.74 G guest instructions and 9.85 G est_cycles (1.716 cycles/insn).
* In the demo1 timedemo window a median frame costs 3.12 M cycles (1.82 M insns). p90 is 4.49 M and max is 9.41 M. At a nominal 100 MHz, the median is 31 ms of CPU work. GPU time and kernel services cost zero in this model.
* Cycles split into 58.3% executed instructions and 41.7% penalties. The largest penalty classes are dependency stalls 9.8%, D-cache misses 8.8%, taken-branch BTB redirects 8.5% and mispredicts 8.2%. I-cache misses cost only 1.6%.
* By subsystem (self cycles, 3 demos): world BSP + edge clip 19.1%, active-edge span generation 18.2%, alias models 15.0%, soft-float helpers 12.7%, GPU submission 12.2%, surface span setup 8.2%, surface cache 4.5%.
* **Soft-float is the cheapest big win.** Double-precision helpers and the double libm kernels cost 13.2% of all cycles. Almost all of it traces to a handful of source lines: an `+ 0.5` in `r_aclip.c` (3.7%), double constants in the inlined `SND_Spatialize` (2.5%), musl `sinf`/`cosf` called by `AngleVectors` (2.7%) and `R_RotateBmodel` (1.0%), `NEAR_CLIP 0.01` in `R_ClipEdge` (1.6%) and `BACKFACE_EPSILON` in the BSP walk (0.6%). See section 7 and target 3, target 4, target 5, target 7, target 18 and target 20.
* **GPU span submission for the viewmodel is the next one.** `flush_span_batch` + `of_emit_span` + their `memset` cost 8.9% of cycles, and 74% of the spans they carry come from `D_PolysetDrawSpans8_NoZ`, the viewmodel. See target 1.
* **Invisible to est_cycles: the silent audio drain.** With the HW mixer, `SNDDMA_FillRing` still pushes 4800 silent PCM pairs per frame through `audio_write` (228,300 calls). The kernel does one uncached SDRAM store per pair, roughly 3.7% of est_cycles at qsim's own IO costs. The fix is a small guard in `snd_of.c`. See target 2.
* **Frame-time spikes come from surface-cache rebuilds.** Mean cost is spread across the world pipeline, but the heaviest frames are dominated by `R_DrawSurfaceBlock8_mip*`: 58% of demo1 flip 34 (9.4 M cycles), 34% of demo1 flip 488 (8.5 M cycles), 54% of demo2 flip 489 (11.7 M cycles). See section 2 and target 10.

Commands (all outputs under `qsim/runs/`):

```sh
cd qsim
for d in 1 2 3; do ./run.sh /home/alberto/Repos/Quake/build/opt-20260915/baseline-obj/app.elf runs/baseline-demo$d demo$d; done
# per-PC penalty-class dump (private instrumented copy of qsim, see below)
make -C analysis/pcx && for d in 1 2 3; do analysis/pcx/qsim_pcx <elf> --pak-dir <id1> --out runs/baseline-demo$d/pcx \
    --args "+timedemo demo$d +host_framerate 0.1" --profile --quiet; done
python3 analysis/make_report.py
```

**Determinism check.** `runs/baseline-demo1` is byte-identical to the integration agent's earlier `runs/baseline_demo1` in `frames.csv` and `profile.tsv`.

**Penalty-class attribution (`analysis/pcx/`).** The stock `profile.tsv` gives self cycles per function but not what the penalty cycles are. `analysis/pcx` is a private copy of the qsim sources. It adds one hook where `cpu_exec.h` already charges `P->pen[last_i]`. The hook snapshots every model penalty counter (`pen_icache`, `pen_dcache`, `pen_dep_by[]`, ...) and a few event counters (uncached-alias and MMIO accesses, load and store misses), and charges the deltas to the same PC. It also writes the op class of each PC (`pcx.tsv`). The shared qsim sources were not modified. Checks:

* the instrumented runs reproduce `frames.csv` and `profile.tsv` byte for byte on all three demos;
* on every PC, the per-class sums equal `pen`;
* per-function aggregates of `pcx.tsv` equal `profile.tsv` (insns, cycles, I-cache, D-cache and mispredict events).

**Scope.** Function profiles cover the whole run, boot and level load included. Load-only functions (`Mod_LoadModel`, `S_LoadSound`, `COM_LoadFile`, and the signon busy-wait in `CL_ParseServerMessage`) therefore appear with small shares. Per-frame statistics use `frames.csv`; the "timedemo window" is the last N flips, where N comes from the result line. "Combined" means the sum over demo1+demo2+demo3.

**Attribution under LTO.** Samples go to ELF symbols. LTO inlined a lot: `R_EmitEdge` into `R_ClipEdge`, the `r_aclip.c` clippers into `R_AliasDrawModel`, `SND_Spatialize` into `S_Update.part.0`, `CL_RelinkEntities` into `_Host_Frame`, and `R_EdgeDrawing`, `R_AnimateLight` and the audio drain into `R_RenderView_`. Where it matters, the text names the inlined source and cites the PC.

The model charges an alias access 16 cycles (`io_access_base` 3 + `io_sdram_uc_extra` 13) and an MMIO access 5; both are GUESS values.

**1. Emit viewmodel spans directly as compact 0x48 lanes, bypassing `span_buf`** (addressable 6.64%, est. saving 3.98%, confidence medium; oracle: pixels identical; `hash_cmd` changes)

`of_emit_span` + `flush_span_batch` + its `memset` cost 881.1 M (8.95%). 1,266,791 of 1,708,551 spans (74%) come from `D_PolysetDrawSpans8_NoZ`, the viewmodel drawn with the CPU edge walker and GPU affine spans (os25 has no PARAM_TRI). Each span is copied (64 B) into `span_buf`, then re-scanned with uint64 overlap tests, many unpredictable branches and a per-group `memset`: about 467 cycles per span before any command word is written. Consecutive scanlines of one triangle never overlap and share flags and texture, so the rasterizer can fill 4-lane records in place (4 + 7N words) with no intermediate buffer. The saving estimate is 60% of the path's viewmodel share.

**2. Skip `audio_drain` with the HW mixer (silent PCM pushed through the kernel every frame)** (addressable 3.81%, est. saving 3.81%, confidence medium; oracle: no rendering impact)

With `SND_USE_HW_MIXER` the 64-pair `snd_buffer` never holds audio. `S_MixerAdvanceTime` still advances `paintedtime` 4800 pairs per 0.1 s frame, and `SNDDMA_FillRing` (called from `R_EdgeDrawing`, inlined in `R_RenderView_`) submits them in 64-pair chunks: 228,300 `audio_write` + as many `audio_get_free` services, about 150 ecalls per frame. The guest loop costs only 9.57 M cycles, but **the kernel side is not in est_cycles**. `of_audio_write` (`openfpgaOS/src/firmware/os/targets/pocket/audio.c:138-165`) does one uncached SDRAM store per pair, plus an MMIO `WPTR` store and ring-position reads per call, and it activates a stream voice that plays the silence. At qsim's own cost parameters (~25 cycles per pair: loop plus a 16-cycle uncached store) that is ~365 M for 14.6 M pairs, excluding trap entry and exit. The fidelity review section of README.md reached the same 2-4% figure independently. Guard `audio_drain` with `#if !SND_USE_HW_MIXER`. The addressable and saving figures here include the kernel estimate, so they are not est_cycles.

**3. Remove the double `+ 0.5` in the alias clippers (`r_aclip.c`)** (addressable 3.65%, est. saving 3.40%, confidence high; oracle: may change clipped alias pixels; verify)

`R_AliasDrawModel` (with `R_AliasClipTriangle` and `R_Alias_clip_*` inlined) makes 1,683,468 calls each to `__extendsfdf2`, `__adddf3` and `__fixdfsi`: est. 359.5 M cycles (3.65%), the largest soft-float consumer. `out->v[i] = pfv0->v[i] + (pfv1->v[i]-pfv0->v[i])*scale + 0.5;` (`r_aclip.c:107,114,130,137,152,159,176,184`). Round in single precision or in integers, e.g. `(int)(x + 0.5f)` or a fixed-point lerp. **Oracle risk:** from 2^23 up, `x + 0.5f` rounds differently from the double sum, which can move s/t/zi by one unit. Check with `compare.py`, or use an exact integer form.

**4. Stop paying musl double-kernel trig in `AngleVectors` / `R_RotateBmodel`** (addressable 3.69%, est. saving 2.96%, confidence medium; oracle: bit-exact if cached; changes pixels if a float kernel is used)

`sinf`/`cosf` average 1,234 and 864 cycles per call including soft-float, because musl evaluates them through double-argument kernels (`__sindf`, `__cosdf`, `__rem_pio2f`). `AngleVectors` (41,597 calls, 3+3 each) costs est. 261.9 M (2.66%) and `R_RotateBmodel` 101.9 M (1.03%). `R_AliasSetUpTransform` makes 77% of the `AngleVectors` calls, once per alias entity per frame. Torches, items and idle monsters keep the same angles from frame to frame, so caching the three vectors per entity, keyed on the angles, is bit-exact. `R_RotateBmodel` can do the same per brush entity. A single-precision sin/cos kernel is faster everywhere but changes pixels. The 80% saving assumes most calls hit the cache.

**5. Make `SND_Spatialize` single precision (`snd_dma.c:358-369`)** (addressable 2.48%, est. saving 2.23%, confidence high; oracle: no rendering impact (mixer volumes may differ by 1))

The inlined copy in `S_Update.part.0` and the out-of-line `SND_Spatialize` make 2,003,702 helper calls: est. 244.4 M cycles (2.48%). `scale = (1.0 - dist) * volume.value` and `(int)(master_vol * scale * (1.0 +/- dot))` are double. Use `1.0f`. The loop also re-spatializes every channel with a sound every frame (~159 double extensions per flip); skipping static channels whose origin and listener did not move would cut the remaining FPU work.

**6. Active-edge span generation (`r_edge.c`): branch and load-stall heavy** (addressable 18.19%, est. saving 1.82%, confidence low; oracle: bit-exact (same spans))

`R_GenerateSpans_Array`, `R_ScanEdges`, `R_TrailingEdge_A` and `R_InsertNewEdges_Array` cost 18.19% of cycles. Their penalties are mostly BTB-taken redirects, mispredicts and load-dependency stalls, not cache misses (section 11). The hottest PC (`R_GenerateSpans_Array+0x94`, `beqz a0`) mispredicts on the surface-key compare. Options: branchless trailing/leading edge selection, fewer taken branches in the u-sort, handle the common case without calling `R_TrailingEdge_A`. Structurally, the GPU already writes world z (Z_WRITE_ZI), so a GPU z-tested world path could drop span sorting entirely. The 10% saving is a guess for micro-optimization only.

**7. `#define NEAR_CLIP 0.01f` (`r_local.h:182`)** (addressable 1.55%, est. saving 1.48%, confidence high; oracle: bit-exact)

`R_ClipEdge` (inlined `R_EmitEdge`, `r_draw.c:114,143`) does 1,767,135 double compares (`__extendsfdf2` + `__ledf2`): est. 153.1 M cycles (1.55%), plus the caller-side double marshalling (the constant is reloaded from the stack for each call). **Bit-exact:** no float lies strictly between 0.01f and 0.01. At z == 0.01f the double compare clamps z to 0.01f, which is its current value. `r_sprite.c:204` and `d_scan.c:764` use the same macro.

**8. Hoist per-triangle overhead out of `D_PolysetDraw` (clean-once, memcpy, call bounce)** (addressable 2.09%, est. saving 1.46%, confidence high; oracle: bit-exact)

`R_AliasPreparePoints` (`r_alias.c:309-329`) calls `D_PolysetDraw` once per triangle with `numtriangles = 1`. Each call runs `of_emit_cache_clean_once` on the skin (hash lookup; 513,986 calls, 40.1 M cycles, 213,339 I-cache misses) and 3 out-of-line `memcpy` of `int[6]` (1,703,064 calls, 86% of all memcpy calls). `D_PolysetDraw` also has the most I-cache misses (407,421). Clean the skin once per model in `R_AliasDrawModel` and copy the three vertices with explicit assignments. Addressable is the clean-once and memcpy share plus `D_PolysetDraw`'s I-cache penalty.

**9. Turbulent (water/lava) surfaces on the CPU: `Turbulent8` + `D_DrawZSpans`** (addressable 2.60%, est. saving 1.30%, confidence low; oracle: changes pixels (GPU warp))

`Turbulent8` samples the warp per pixel into a scratch row, then cleans the row and emits a span (7,770 surface calls, 185.1 M cycles). `D_DrawZSpans` fills z through the uncached alias for the same surfaces (71.1 M, 54% uncached IO). demo2 (4.01%) is the water-heavy case. Rendering a warped 64x64 texture once per frame and drawing it with the param-span path (Z_WRITE_ZI) would move both to the GPU.

**10. Surface-cache rebuilds and their GPU visibility barrier (the frame-time spikes)** (addressable 4.49%, est. saving 1.12%, confidence medium; oracle: bit-exact (unless GPU lighting))

`R_DrawSurfaceBlock8_mip0/1/2` cost 3.83% on average. Publishing each rebuilt block (`of_emit_cache_clean` inlined in `D_DrawSpans8`: cbo.flush plus a re-read of every line) costs another 65.1 M. By construction every read-back is a D-cache miss (`D_DrawSpans8+0x117c`). **They also make the worst frames** (section 2): the rebuilds are 58% of demo1 flip 34 (9.4 M cycles), 34% of demo1 flip 488 (8.5 M cycles), 54% of demo2 flip 489 (11.7 M cycles). Options: a larger surface cache (fewer rebuilds, `SURFCACHE_HEAP_FRACTION`), a single flush per frame over a contiguous dirty range instead of a per-block re-read, amortizing rebuilds across frames, or GPU-side lighting (`D_GPU_WORLD_LIGHT`, which currently disables the param-list path).

**11. Per-surface span-list setup (`D_DrawSurfaces`, `D_CalcGradients`, `D_DrawSpans8`, `of_emit_param_span_list`)** (addressable 6.26%, est. saving 0.94%, confidence low; oracle: bit-exact)

616.4 M (6.26%), excluding the cache-clean barrier (target 10). `D_DrawSpans8` walks the span linked list twice (bounds, then records), and `of_emit_param_span_list` calls `flush_span_batch` on entry (173,153 calls; 94% of all `flush_span_batch` calls return with nothing queued, after building a 720-byte stack frame). One pass that computes bounds while filling records, plus an inline `span_buf_count` check before the call, removes most of this. D-cache penalties dominate `D_DrawSurfaces` (44% of its cycles; `surf_t` and `msurface_t` touches).

**12. Cheapen `flush_span_batch` for the other span sources (HUD blits, turbulent rows)** (addressable 2.31%, est. saving 0.93%, confidence medium; oracle: pixels identical; `hash_cmd` may change)

The span path cost left after target 1. Drop the per-group `memset` of the 8-lane `of_gpu_affine_span_group_t` (write only the lanes used), compare 32-bit ranges instead of uint64, and pre-classify spans when enqueuing. Mispredicts plus BTB redirects are 24% of `flush_span_batch` cycles. HUD blits (`Draw_Pic`, `Draw_TransPic`, `Draw_Character`) emit one span per row; a column or rect command per pic would remove most of them.

**13. World BSP/surface data locality (`R_RenderFace`, `R_RecursiveWorldNode`, `D_DrawSurfaces`, bmodels)** (addressable 2.54%, est. saving 0.89%, confidence medium; oracle: bit-exact)

These functions pay 250.1 M D-cache penalty cycles (2.54%). The hottest miss PCs are the first touch of the surface record (`R_RenderFace+0x12 lw a0,24(a0)`, 88% miss rate), the surfedge list and edge records (`R_RenderFace+0x19c/+0x1b0`), `mnode_t` children, and stack reloads during the recursion (`lw ra,92(sp)`). Split hot/cold fields (visframe, flags, plane, first edge, numedges) into dense arrays, or touch the surface record early when a node is accepted, so its refill overlaps other work.

**14. Sparse-array scans that miss the D-cache: `cl_entities` loop and `R_AnimateLight`** (addressable 1.03%, est. saving 0.82%, confidence high; oracle: bit-exact)

The `cl_entities` loop of `CL_RelinkEntities` (inlined in `_Host_Frame`, `cl_main.c:481-487`) loads `ent->model` (`_Host_Frame+0x173c lw a5,48(s6)`) and, for empty slots, `ent->forcelink` (`+0x2064`), which sit on two different cache lines of each 180-byte `entity_t`. It iterates 853,355 times (~272 slots per flip), takes 1,206,437 load misses and costs 85.1 M penalty cycles, while doing about six instructions of work per slot. `R_AnimateLight` (inlined in `R_RenderView_` at `+0x1c0`) loads `cl_lightstyle[j].length` for all 64 styles every frame, one 68-byte record per iteration: 195,370 misses and 16.2 M penalty cycles, plus a `rem` per style. Total 101.3 M (1.03%). Keep a dense list of occupied entity slots and a packed array of style lengths.

**15. Alias CPU raster: z-test and pixel writes through the uncached alias** (addressable 1.51%, est. saving 0.75%, confidence low; oracle: bit-exact if logic preserved)

`D_PolysetDrawSpans8` spends 33% of its cycles on uncached-IO penalty (7,119,244 alias accesses: z reads, z writes, pixel writes) and 10% on skin/colormap D-cache misses. Options: z-reject whole spans against a cached coarse z, batch pixel runs, or keep a CPU-cached z mirror for alias occlusion. The world z is GPU-written, so any mirror needs a sync point. The alias cost is a GUESS parameter (16 cycles; anomaly A3), so check on hardware before investing.

**16. `memcpy`/`memset` call overhead for tiny fixed sizes** (addressable 1.32%, est. saving 0.66%, confidence medium; oracle: bit-exact)

`memcpy` 169.8 M and `memset` 90.7 M combined. Most calls are small fixed sizes from hot paths: the `int[6]` vertex copies in `D_PolysetDraw`, the 8-lane group `memset` in `flush_span_batch` (324,494 calls), and the persp builder `memset`. `memset` pays 31% of its cycles in store-buffer-full REDOs, and `memcpy` has 242,081 I-cache misses. Inline the fixed sizes; the SDK build does not expand `__builtin_memcpy` of 24 bytes (`D_PolysetDraw` calls `memcpy`).

**17. Fewer command words (CPU-ring MMIO upload costs ~7.6 cycles per word)** (addressable 2.20%, est. saving 0.66%, confidence medium; oracle: pixels identical; `hash_cmd` changes)

`_gpu_flush_cmd_stream` costs 216.8 M (2.20%) for 28,390,822 words. os25 has no command DMA, so the only lever is fewer words: fuller affine lanes (the 8-lane group is split into 4-lane 0x48 commands, so the header repeats), no 1-lane groups from per-row HUD blits, fewer long-form headers for world span lists (0x58 continuation is not on os25). On hardware each MMIO store is a single-outstanding AXI transaction, possibly slower than the 5-cycle GUESS.

**18. Sweep the remaining double literals (network coords, view bob, palette, sky)** (addressable 0.93%, est. saving 0.65%, confidence high; oracle: bit-exact for `0.125f`; view math may change pixels)

`MSG_ReadCoord`/`CL_Parse*` `* (1.0/8)` (41.9 M, bit-exact as `0.125f`), the view setup inlined into `SCR_UpdateScreen` (bob/roll `sinf` and double constants), `V_AddIdle`, `V_UpdatePalette` `powf`, `D_DrawSprite`, `D_Sky_uv_To_st`: 91.8 M combined (0.93%). Building with `-Wdouble-promotion` lists them all. `-fsingle-precision-constant` fixes them wholesale, but check the oracle.

**19. `R_ClipEdge`: FPU-result stalls and per-vertex `1/z` divides** (addressable 2.11%, est. saving 0.63%, confidence low; oracle: bit-exact if same math)

3,086,147 fdiv (52,464,499 freeze cycles) and 155,610,691 stall cycles waiting on fmadd/fsub results (26% of the function). Quake caches emitted edges per frame only when they are unclipped (`cachededgeoffset`). A frame-stamped per-vertex cache of `1/z` and screen u/v would skip the transform and divide for vertices shared by adjacent clipped edges. Reordering statements to avoid read-after-fmadd stalls is free, but the FPU latencies are GUESS values (anomaly A2).

**20. `#define BACKFACE_EPSILON 0.01f` (`r_local.h:78`)** (addressable 0.64%, est. saving 0.61%, confidence high; oracle: bit-exact)

`R_RecursiveWorldNode` and `R_DrawBEntitiesOnList` make 594,074 double compares (`r_bsp.c:432,510,661,670`): est. 63.0 M (0.64%). **Bit-exact** for `<`/`>` against +/-0.01, because 0.01f rounds toward zero, so no float lies between the two thresholds on the side that matters. The double constants are also stored on the stack (`R_RecursiveWorldNode` `lw a2,0(sp)` is a D-cache miss PC).

**21. Code layout / I-cache (low priority in the model)** (addressable 1.60%, est. saving 0.48%, confidence low; oracle: bit-exact)

I-cache penalty is only 1.60%, with 2,759,041 misses. The hot set fits: 99% of instructions come from 47 KB of code. Hardware history argues against BRAM placement (`quakedef.h:135-143`: BRAM placement and hot clustering both regressed on the device; anomalies A4/A5). Fix the per-triangle call bounce first (target 8), then re-measure.

**22. Load-then-use stalls in the edge/span inner loops** (addressable 1.37%, est. saving 0.41%, confidence low; oracle: bit-exact)

Load-dependency stalls total 5.47% of cycles. The model charges up to 2 cycles when an instruction reads a register loaded by the one before. `R_TrailingEdge_A` stalls on 30% of its cycles, `R_GenerateSpans_Array` on 12%, `D_DrawSpans8` on 12%. Hoist loads of loop invariants and interleave independent work between a load and its use (the `sort_keys[]` comment in `r_edge.c` shows this already paid off once). Addressable here is a quarter of all load stalls.

**23. `R_LightPoint` per alias entity per frame (`RecursiveLightPoint`)** (addressable 0.65%, est. saving 0.39%, confidence medium; oracle: bit-exact if invalidated correctly)

71,981 recursive calls, 64.2 M cycles, 1.23 D-cache penalty cycles per instruction and 20% FPU-result stalls. Cache the light value per entity while its origin, the lightstyle values and the dlights are unchanged.

**24. Branchless alias edge walker and span loops (`D_PolysetScanLeftEdge`, `D_PolysetDrawSpans8*`)** (addressable 0.98%, est. saving 0.39%, confidence medium; oracle: bit-exact)

The DDA `if (errorterm >= 0)` and `if (d_tfrac & 0x10000)` in `D_PolysetScanLeftEdge` (`d_polyse.c`) and the z-test / `lcount` loop tests in the span drawers mispredict 8,416,458 times. Mispredict plus BTB-taken penalties are 96.3 M (0.98%): 16% of `D_PolysetScanLeftEdge`, 14% of `D_PolysetDrawSpans8` and 17% of `D_PolysetDrawSpans8_NoZ`. Select the base/extra steps with a mask (`m = errorterm >> 31`) and fold the `tfrac` carry arithmetically (`d_ptex += (d_tfrac >> 16) * skinwidth; d_tfrac &= 0xFFFF`).

**25. `R_AliasDrawModel` vertex transform/projection (beyond the soft-float fix)** (addressable 1.17%, est. saving 0.35%, confidence low; oracle: bit-exact if same math)

Freezes on 1,342,768 fdiv (22,827,056 cycles), FPU-result stalls 55,507,453, D-cache 36,402,106 (vertex, normal and stvert tables). The viewmodel is drawn every frame with mostly identical transforms between frames, so a per-model frame cache of projected verts keyed on frame/angles/origin would help. `R_AliasCheckBBox` (1,105 cycles per entity, 8 corners projected) could early-out on a bounding sphere first.

The estimated savings add up to 33.1% of cycles. Some overlap (target 1, target 12 and target 16; target 8 and target 16; target 6 and target 22), so the sum is not a projection. The six soft-float targets (3, 4, 5, 7, 18, 20) alone are est. 11.3%, and four of them are bit-exact or have no rendering impact.

## 14. Anomalies and model caveats

Items that look like simulator or model issues, or that limit how far the numbers can be trusted:

* **A1. Reporting bug: `softfloat_calls` in `frames.csv`/`summary.json` double-counts aliased helpers.** `main.c` builds `sf_idx` from every soft-float STT_FUNC symbol. libgcc exports `__ltdf2`/`__ledf2` (0x1046cb22), `__gtdf2`/`__gedf2` (0x1046ca80), `__eqdf2`/`__nedf2` and the tf2 pairs at the same address, so those calls are counted twice. Combined, `summary.json` reports 17,089,376 soft-float calls and the profile helper table 14,463,403. The difference, 2,625,973, equals the calls into the aliased entry points (2,625,973). Fix: dedupe `sf_idx` by address. The profile tables are right; they name each alias pair by one symbol, so calls to `__ledf2`/`__gedf2` show up as `__ltdf2`/`__gtdf2`.
* **A2. The FPU result latencies are GUESS values and they drive 3.77% of cycles.** With `lat_fadd` 6 and `lat_fma` 6, any instruction that reads an fadd/fmadd result within 6 cycles stalls. `R_ClipEdge` has 7.3 M stall cycles on a single `fmadd.s` PC in demo1. Pin these parameters down (VexiiRiscv FPU plugin stage settings) before chasing statement order in FPU code.
* **A3. The uncached alias costs 16 cycles per access (GUESS), MMIO 5 (GUESS).** 3.37% of cycles depend on them: the alias z/FB raster and the CPU-ring command upload. Neither SDRAM arbiter contention with the GPU nor GPU backpressure is modelled, so real alias and ring costs could be much higher.
* **A4. BRAM refill cost is a GUESS, and hardware history says it is expensive.** The vendored `FetchL1Plugin.scala` builds the fetch PMA from all executable regions and flags BRAM refills `isIo`, so qsim's cached-BRAM fetch agrees with the RTL. The `app.ld` comment calling APP_BRAM uncached is stale. But `bram_setup` 3 / `bram_per_word` 1 are GUESS values, and `PQ_FASTTEXT` BRAM placement cost 35% of frame time on the device (`quakedef.h:135-143`). io-path refills are probably far slower than modelled. This ELF has no BRAM code, so the baseline is unaffected, but BRAM-placement experiments in qsim would be optimistic.
* **A5. I-cache sensitivity looks low next to hardware history.** The model charges 1.60% for I-cache misses (about 54 cycles per miss). Hot-function clustering cost 45 ms per frame on the device. Either real misses are much more expensive (fetch refills competing with GPU texture DMA on the shared SDRAM arbiter is not modelled), or the regression had another cause. Treat layout conclusions from qsim as weak.
* **A6. D-cache load-miss cost.** 78 penalty cycles per load miss: REDO 9 + refill + a serialized dirty-victim writeback (GUESS). The entity-loop and BSP targets are sensitive to that GUESS.
* **A7. Kernel services cost zero.** 228,300 `audio_write` calls alone (the silent drain, target 2) have no cost in the model, although the kernel does an uncached store per PCM pair. The `SYS_CYCLE` counter is instret x 3, so the signon busy wait's length is not the device's. Signon only; it does not affect the timedemo window.
* **A8. Attribution under LTO.** `R_RenderView_` (11.5 KB) contains `R_EdgeDrawing`, `R_AnimateLight`, the audio drain and the `R_DrawEntitiesOnList` loop. `_Host_Frame` contains `CL_RelinkEntities`. Per-symbol shares for these two are mixtures (section 1).
* **Checked, not an anomaly: 8-cycle charge for `jal` on a BTB miss.** The vendored VexiiRiscv has no decode-stage prediction plugin (`prediction/` holds only Btb, GShare, History and Learn), and `BranchPlugin` resolves JAL in execute, so a BTB-missed direct jump pays the full flush. 7,128,236 of 36,906,246 returns (19%) mispredict because the RAS is only updated on BTB hits, as in `BtbPlugin.scala`. The predictor sizes match `configs/os25.cfg` (BTB 256 sets, GShare 1 KB).
* **No functional anomalies.** No traps, no unmapped MMIO, no misaligned accesses (counter 0), the BRAM I-cache counter is 0 as expected, and the per-PC class sums reconcile exactly with `pen` on every PC.
